## Originating intent

Describe the project in one paragraph without implementation details.

Answer:

- What condition should change?
- For whom or what?

- What outcome would count?
- What outcome would not count?

## Success questions

```text
What is true?
What is authoritative?
What is unresolved?
What is Ready?
What is blocked and why?
What evidence supports current claims?
What effects are currently allowed?
What requires a human decision?
```
